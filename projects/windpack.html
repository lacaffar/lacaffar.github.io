Where this page stands

This is the project I have the most to say about and the least of it written down. What follows is an outline. The design decisions, the test data, and the photos from the shop are all still to come.

Overview

Windpack is NC State's team for the U.S. Department of Energy Collegiate Wind Competition. I founded it in August 2025 and it has grown to more than 25 members across mechanical, electrical, and project development sub-teams. We placed 6th of 10 at our first national competition in 2026.

The competition asks for two things at once: a scaled turbine that performs under test, and a project development case you defend in front of judges.

This season

Off printed parts, onto machined ones

I use my senior design lab TA position to train club members on the mills and lathes, which moved the turbine off last year's 3D printed parts and onto machined precision components.

Composite blades

Every other team ran 3D printed blades. We laid up a composite blade with inlaid glass fiber in epoxy, then ran a design trade study with a group of materials science students to settle which construction is actually the better choice. That study is not finished.

Controls

Helping lead the controls team on a Simulink PID loop running on an Arduino that feathers the blades and modulates generator resistance against wind speed and other measured inputs.

CAD practice

I own the team's SolidWorks practice: assembly structure, part numbering, and interface definition between the mechanical and electrical sub-teams, so their hardware integrates on first fit.
