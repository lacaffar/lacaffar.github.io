Windpack

NC State's student club designing, building, and testing scaled wind turbines for the Collegiate Wind Competition. Founded in fall 2025 with ten of us and a whiteboard.

Overview

Windpack is NC State's team for the Collegiate Wind Competition. I founded the club in fall 2025 and serve as president, which on a ten-person team means everything from recruiting and sponsor outreach to making sure the turbine actually gets built.

The competition asks for two things at once: a scaled turbine that performs under test, and a project-development case you defend in front of judges. We took our first turbine to competition in 2026.

The build

Design

Turbine architecture, blade geometry, and generator matching, with every choice caught between performance and what a student team can actually manufacture.

Fabrication

How the turbine actually got built: printing, machining, assembly, and the parts that fought back.

Testing

Test-stand time. Find what fails first, fix it, spin it again.
