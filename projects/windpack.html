Overview

Windpack is NC State's team for the Collegiate Wind Competition. I founded the club in fall 2025 with ten of us and a whiteboard, and serve as president, which on a team that size means everything from recruiting and sponsor outreach to making sure the turbine actually gets built.

The competition asks for two things at once: a scaled turbine that performs under test, and a project-development case you defend in front of judges. We took our first turbine to competition in 2026.
