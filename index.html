About

I study mechanical engineering at NC State, with an economics minor, and I prefer hardware I can put my hands on.

As a freshman I spent a semester in the FPAC lab on KSTAR fusion diagnostics. In 2025 I founded Windpack, NC State's Collegiate Wind Competition team, and still run it as president. It has its own site at windpack.club. Summer 2026 I was an engineering intern in fleet performance management at GE Vernova. This year I am a teaching assistant in the senior design shop, training students on the mills, lathes, waterjet, and the rest of the machines.

Off the clock: beach volleyball, weekend shifts at a German bakery, and two years riding 911 calls as an EMS cadet. That last one reset my definition of pressure.
