About

I'm a mechanical engineering student at NC State (GPA 3.889), and I like hardware you can put your hands on. Freshman year I sent a long-shot email to a fusion lab and ended up spending a semester processing spectroscopy data from KSTAR reactor experiments.

Last fall I started Windpack, NC State's team for the Collegiate Wind Competition, and I'm still its president. This summer I'm in Greenville, SC as a wind engineer intern at GE Vernova, working on utility-scale turbines. On my own bench, I'm building a high-power rocket avionics stack, mostly because live telemetry off something moving that fast looked just hard enough to be interesting.

Off the clock: beach volleyball, and weekend shifts at a German bakery in Cary. In high school I was an EMS cadet riding along on 911 calls, which permanently reset my definition of "high pressure."
