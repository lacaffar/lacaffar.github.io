Overview

I spent summer 2026 in fleet performance management on the onshore wind side of GE Vernova. The role was undefined when I arrived, so I scoped it myself: I went to platform, controls, and reliability engineers, asked what was actually slowing them down, and turned that into five projects I could finish before August.

What I worked on

Blade serial numbers out of drone imagery

A Python tool that pulls drone inspection imagery, reads the blade serial number off it with a vision model, and reconciles what it finds against fleet records. The inspection vendor had quoted fifty thousand dollars for the same capability.

Controller fault triage

Automated turbine controller fault triage in Python and SQL, routing qualifying flags to controls or platform engineering. Saved three to six engineer hours a week.

Measurement system analysis

Ran an MSA across two fleet databases to settle which source forced controller variables should be measured from, and corrected 54 erroneous turbine configuration records along the way.
